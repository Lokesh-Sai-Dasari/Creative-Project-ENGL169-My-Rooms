# Sound credits

Recorded sounds in `assets/sfx/`:

- fire, crickets, owl, birds, wind, rain, keyboard, paper, riffle, fan, bubbles, drops: from the Moodist project (github.com/remvze/moodist), sounds under the Pixabay Content License / CC0.
- laugh, snore, cough, knock, creak, creakdoor, can, sip, pour, sigh: from the ESC-50 dataset (K. J. Piczak, github.com/karolpiczak/ESC-50), clips originally from Freesound.org, CC BY-NC. Clips used: 3-126113-A-26, 1-53444-A-28, 2-87795-A-24, 3-182023-A-30, 4-160036-A-33, 5-161270-A-33, 2-130245-A-34, 4-188191-C-29, 2-126433-A-17, 2-54962-A-23.

All other sounds are synthesized in the browser. The voice recordings are my own.
